Study route: fourteen weeks

The course joins three bodies of mathematics that already exist in separate editions: continuum kinematics, flow-matching generative models, and the statistical theory of turbulence. Eleven weeks read chapters of those editions. Three weeks, and the course map, are new chapters written for this course. Two laboratories turn the mathematics into trained models that are judged by physical statistics.

How to use this route. Read the course map and notation chapter first, because the five source editions use different symbols for the same objects. In each linked week, read the named chapter of the source edition, then return here for the next week. The linked chapters are not copied: the source edition remains the authoritative text.

Linked chapters belong to the owner's independent study editions of IIT Madras Continuum Mechanics (Gaurav Bhutani), IIT Kanpur Fluid Mechanics, MIT 6.S184 Flow Matching and Diffusion Models, IIT Bombay Essentials of Turbulence, and IIT Madras Turbulence Modeling. A read-only depth check matched each linked chapter to the learning outcome it supports; the result is recorded in the table below. The check read chapter text and code, but it is not an independent review of those chapters.

3. Pace for a reader with mathematical physics

Part A is mostly revision for a reader who already knows vector calculus and PDEs. Weeks 1–3 can be read in about two weeks if the reader concentrates on the deformation gradient and on the determinant rate $\dot J=J\,\mathrm{div}\,\mathbf v$, because chapter 2 builds its probability argument on exactly those results. The time saved is best spent on the two laboratories, which take the longest to run and to interpret.

4. Assessment

Each outcome is assessed in the week the table names. Derivation outcomes (2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 13, 17) are assessed by the derivation exercises of the corresponding chapter. Computational outcomes (7, 11, 14, 15) are assessed by the laboratories and by the programs of chapters 3 and 5. The integrative outcome 18 is assessed by the capstone study in chapter 6, which has its own rubric.

5. What this route does not claim

The route does not make the linked chapters part of this edition, and it does not certify them. The depth check found two gaps that the new chapters fill (the probability form of the continuity equation, and the link between a Gaussian filter and the heat equation) and one gap that a laboratory fills (a two-dimensional neural flow-matching model). The course does not teach how to write a flow solver or how to engineer large-scale GPU training.
