Transport, Continua and Generative Models of Turbulent Flow

A synthesis course: continuum kinematics, flow matching and turbulence statistics, joined by new bridge chapters and laboratories.

All 6 new chapters have passed two independent scientific reviews. The public completion and publication records state the current release status, tested scope and limitations.

The fourteen weeks

The course has fourteen weeks and six new chapters. Eleven weeks read chapters of five existing study editions. The table links every reading in order. The linked chapters stay on their own sites and do not link back: return to this page for the next week. The study route adds the parts, the pace and the assessment.
